C01, LabStaff Represents laboratory staff who perform equipment checkouts, process returns, and monitor overdue equipment. UC-13, UC-15, UC-17

C02, LoanRecord Stores loan transaction details including borrower information, checkout dates, return deadlines, and overdue status. UC-13, UC-17

C03, ReturnTransaction Processes the physical return of equipment, verifies return dates against deadlines, and logs the receiving staff member. UC-15

C04, Notification Handles automated alerts and reminders sent to borrowers for overdue equipment and loan updates. UC-17, UC-18

C05, Equipment Maintains equipment specifications, current physical condition, and real-time availability status. UC-13, UC-15
