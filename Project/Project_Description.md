Manual tracking of lab equipment might lead into schedule conflicts, lost items and might containts a lot of human error.
This software is  designed for: equipment borrowers, lab facutlty and assistants
Main Features: digitizes the equipment lifecycle, replacing manual logs with automated inventory tracking and accountability workflows.
Key functionalities: 
 - Secure, role-based login for different users.
 - Searchable catalog showing real-time equipment availability.
 - Digital reporting to log item damage.
 - Automated email alerts for overdue items.
