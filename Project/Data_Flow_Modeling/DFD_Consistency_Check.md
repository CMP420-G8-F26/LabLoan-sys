The Context Diagram and Level-0 DFD focus on the three primary entities directly involved in managing laboratory equipment loans and returns: Student, Admin, and Lab Staff.

Faculty/Instructor was identified as a stakeholder in Lab 3 because instructors may need to search for equipment and check its availability. However, these activities use the same equipment-search and availability functions already represented in the DFD.

For this reason, Faculty/Instructor is not modeled as a separate external entity in the current diagrams. The DFDs focus on the primary operational roles involved in equipment management, loan requests, checkout, returns, and reporting.
